Our process

How we design.

We treat CAD as part of the build, not a formality. Every model has to survive fabrication, assembly, and a full competition weekend.

  1. 01

    Sketch the problem

    Break the game down, define requirements, and sketch concepts on paper first.

  2. 02

    Model the subsystem

    Build parametric parts in Onshape with real hardware dimensions and clear mates.

  3. 03

    Validate and prototype

    Check for interference, print the critical pieces, and test before committing.

  4. 04

    Export and document

    Publish STEP and STL files with names that make sense to the next person.

File standards

Easy to find. Easy to use.
