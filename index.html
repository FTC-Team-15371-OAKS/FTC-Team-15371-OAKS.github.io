Who we are

A student-led team with an engineer's mindset.

OAKS Robotics is the FIRST Tech Challenge team at Norfolk Collegiate. We are students from across the school who share one thing: the urge to figure out how things work — and then make them work better.

Every season starts with a new game and a blank page. We break down the challenge, sketch ideas, model parts, write code, and test until the robot can hold its own on the field. Along the way, we learn how to communicate, collaborate, and solve problems as a team.
