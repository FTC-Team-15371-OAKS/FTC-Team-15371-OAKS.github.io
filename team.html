How we work

Student-led, mentor-supported.

Our mentors don't build the robot for us. They teach the tools, ask hard questions, and help us find the answer when we're stuck. The design decisions, code, and competition strategy belong to the students.

That means everyone learns something they didn't know in September — whether it's parametric CAD, Java, wiring, machining, or how to explain a design choice to a judge.
